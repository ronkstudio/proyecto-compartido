93|With your bank card (OVpay)
94|On trams, buses, the metro and trains across the country you can tap your contactless debit or credit card, or your phone, on the reader when you get on and again when you get off. No need to register or download any app.
95|Always check out
96|You have to tap the same card when you get on and when you get off. If you forget to check out, you're charged a high fare.
97|One card per person
98|Each person needs their own card or phone: two people can't travel on the same card.
99|GVB day tickets
100|If you'll travel a lot, there are tickets for 1 to 7 days with unlimited travel on GVB trams, metro and buses. They're sold in the GVB app, at the machines and at GVB offices. They're not valid on trains.

149|City of Amsterdam (city information)
150|Amsterdam is a safe city, but there are pickpockets wherever it's crowded.
151|It happens mainly at the central station, on crowded trams, on Dam Square, on the Damrak, in the Red Light District and at markets.
152|Wear your backpack on your front and don't leave your phone on the table at terraces.
153|If you rent a bike, always lock it: bike theft is the most common crime of all.
154|The real danger: bikes
155|The most common cause of tourist accidents is walking on the bike lane: it's red and runs next to the pavement.
156|Look both ways before crossing, for bikes and trams too, which are silent.
157|The canals have no railings: at night, and if you've been drinking, stay away from the edge.
158|Scams and things to avoid
159|People offering drugs in the street sell fake or dangerous stuff: never buy.
160|Fake police officers: plain-clothes people who ask to see your wallet. Real police don't do that.
161|Stolen bikes at bargain prices: buying a stolen bike is a crime.
162|Currency exchange: avoid street exchange offices, which charge a lot. Better to pay by card.
163|Rules in the centre
164|In the Red Light District you can't smoke cannabis in the street and it's forbidden to photograph the sex workers.
165|In many parts of the centre you can't drink alcohol in the street, and urinating in public is fined.
166|Coffeeshops sell cannabis only to over-18s, with ID. What you buy there can't be taken out of the country.
167|Guided tours past the windows of the Red Light District are banned.
168|Carry only what you need for the day: one card, some cash and a copy of your ID.
169|If you're robbed, call the police on 0900 8844 or go to a police station to report it.
170|If your passport is stolen: almost all embassies are in The Hague, an hour away by train; some countries have a consulate in Amsterdam. They're on the map under Info > Help.
171|Get travel insurance: medical care is good but expensive without cover.
172|You can pay by card almost everywhere, contactless and by phone too. Many places don't take cash: you'll see "pin only" or "no cash" signs.
173|Watch out with credit cards
174|Some supermarkets and small shops only accept debit cards. Bring both.
175|Use bank ATMs. If the ATM or card reader offers to charge you in your own currency, always choose euros.
176|It isn't compulsory. People usually round up or leave a little extra if the service was good.
177|Accommodation charges an extra tax as a percentage of the room price, one of the highest in Europe. Ask whether it's already included.
