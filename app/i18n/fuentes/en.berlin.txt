90|U-Bahn and S-Bahn
91|The U-Bahn is the metro, mostly underground. The S-Bahn is the urban train, above ground. You can switch between them without paying again.
92|From Monday to Thursday they close for a few hours in the early morning. On Friday and Saturday nights they run all night.
93|Lines S41 and S42 go all the way round the centre: handy for changing neighbourhood without going through Mitte.
94|Look at the platform sign: it shows the last station on the line, which is the direction of the train.
