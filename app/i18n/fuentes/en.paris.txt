65|To get to or from Charles de Gaulle or Orly by RER, metro or bus you need the Airports ticket (Paris Région <> Aéroports).
66|At station machines and ticket offices, or in the official Île-de-France Mobilités app, which loads tickets onto your phone.

72|Metro and RER
73|The metro has 16 numbered lines with very frequent trains. The RER are fast trains with letters (A, B, C, D, E) that cross the city and reach the suburbs.
74|The metro runs from early morning until after midnight, a bit later on Fridays and Saturdays. Check before heading back late.
75|Finding your way
76|Each direction is named after the line's final station. Follow the signs with the line number and the name of that last station.
77|Changing lines
78|Big stations like Châtelet have very long corridors. If you have luggage, look for a simpler connection.
79|Accessibility
80|Many metro stations only have stairs. Line 14 and much of the RER have lifts.
81|Buses and trams
82|They cover the whole city and let you see Paris through the window. Validate your ticket when boarding, at the machine next to the driver.
83|When the metro closes, the Noctilien night buses (with the letter N) take over. Several leave from Châtelet and the main stations.
84|They circle the city along the outer boulevards. Useful for going from one edge to another without passing through the centre.
85|Use Google Maps, Citymapper or the Île-de-France Mobilités app: they show the line, the stop and the waiting time.

94|They have a "TAXI PARISIEN" sign on the roof: green light means free, red means taken. Catch one at a rank, in the street or via an app.
95|From the airports

97|Uber, Bolt, FREENOW, Heetch and G7 work across the city and show the price before the ride.
98|At the airport use only the official taxi rank. Don't accept offers from people who approach you in the terminal.

104|Safety on public transport
105|It happens mostly on crowded metro and RER trains (line 1 and the RER B to the airport), on platforms and in busy places.
106|Wear your backpack on your front and keep pockets closed, especially when getting on and off as the doors close.
107|At terraces and cafés, don't leave your phone on the table or your bag hanging on the chair.

109|Paris is safe to explore; these are places where it pays to be more alert, especially at night.

116|Petitions: they ask you to sign for a charity and then demand a donation, or use it to rob you.
