106|10-trip ticket (T-casual)
107|The most used: 10 trips on metro, buses, tram and trains within zone 1, transfers included. It's for one person only: if two of you travel, each needs your own. It's not valid at the airport metro stations.

151|City information (only from within Barcelona)
152|Barcelona is famous for its pickpockets: they're very skilled and work in groups. It isn't dangerous, but you need to stay alert all the time.
153|It happens mostly on La Rambla, the metro (lines L3 and L1, at Liceu, Catalunya, Passeig de Gràcia and Sagrada Família), the Sagrada Família, Park Güell and Barceloneta beach.
154|Carry your backpack in front and keep pockets closed. At terraces, don't leave your phone on the table or your bag hanging on the chair.
155|On the beach, never leave things alone on your towel while you go into the water.
156|These are places where it's worth being more alert, especially at night.
157|La Rambla and Plaça Reial, at all hours.
158|Some streets of El Raval at night, especially towards the port.
159|Barceloneta beach and Port Olímpic when the clubs let out.
160|Metro platforms and escalators at the most touristy stations.
161|The shell game: guessing which cup the ball is under, especially on La Rambla. The winners are accomplices: don't bet.
162|The stain or bird poo trick: someone stains your clothes and offers to clean you up while someone else takes your wallet.
163|The footballer: someone stops you to show you a move or hugs you over a match, and meanwhile goes through your pockets.
164|Fake police officers: plain-clothes people who ask to see your wallet or passport. Real police don't do that on the street.
165|Petitions: they ask you to sign for a cause and then demand a donation, or use it to rob you.
166|On the road: if someone signals that you have a flat tyre, don't stop on the hard shoulder; keep going to a petrol station.
167|With these precautions, the vast majority of visits go by without any trouble.
168|If you're robbed, file a report at a Mossos d'Esquadra station or at the Guàrdia Urbana tourist assistance service (La Rambla 43), which has interpreters.
169|Accommodation charges a separate tax per person per night, combining Catalonia's and the city's. Ask whether it's already included in the price.
